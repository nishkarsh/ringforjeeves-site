You already have something that answers questions. What you do not have is anyone who notices.

Nobody tells you the rail pass lapses a fortnight on Thursday with a leg unused. Nobody remembers you promised a colleague those dates on Monday and it is now Thursday. Nobody looks at tomorrow, sees no gap for lunch, and says so tonight.

That is the work of a butler. Jeeves performs it from your own Mac: he reads what you allow him to read, keeps his own notes on your affairs, and speaks first.
